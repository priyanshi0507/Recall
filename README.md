
# Recall 🧠
### Learn Smarter. Remember Longer.

**Recall** is an AI-powered targeted learning platform designed to help learners retain what they learn through intelligent, personalized revision.

Inspired by how human memory works, Recall identifies what you are likely to forget and reminds you of it at the right time. Instead of simply reminding you to revise, it creates a smarter learning experience by recommending curated articles, practice problems, and targeted learning resources based on your needs.

With continuous performance assessment, Recall adapts to your learning patterns and automatically determines **what to revisit, when to revisit it, and how often** — helping you focus on what matters most.

**The goal:** Turn everyday learning into long-term knowledge.
